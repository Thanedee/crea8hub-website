06Questions We Get Asked

Social Media Marketing & Management FAQs

How much does social media management cost in Nigeria?

Social media management pricing in Nigeria depends on how many platforms you need, how many posts and videos you want each month, whether community management is daily or weekly, and whether paid advertising is included. A single-platform package costs far less than a full multi-platform retainer with video production. Tell us your goals and we'll send a package and price that fits.

What's the difference between social media management and social media marketing?

Social media management is the day-to-day running of your pages: planning, posting and replying. Social media marketing includes that, plus strategy, campaigns, paid advertising and influencer work aimed at specific business results such as leads or sales. We offer both.

Do you create the content or do we?

We create it. Our team writes, designs and edits your content, and we can shoot photos and video too. You approve everything before it goes live, and you're welcome to send us product updates, events or news to include.

How soon will we see results?

You'll see more consistent activity and engagement within the first month. Follower growth and enquiries from organic content build over three to six months; paid social can drive traffic and leads within days of launch.

Do you only work with businesses in Lagos?

We're based in Lagos, but we manage social media for brands across Nigeria. Most of our work happens online, and we travel for shoots and events when needed.

Which social media platform is best for my business in Nigeria?

It depends on who your customers are. Instagram and TikTok suit consumer and lifestyle brands, Facebook reaches broad audiences across Nigeria, LinkedIn suits B2B and professional services, and X suits brands that thrive on conversation and customer care. We'll recommend where to focus after reviewing your audience and goals.
