06Questions We Get Asked

Fintech & Financial Services Marketing FAQs

Do you understand advertising rules for financial services in Nigeria?

Yes. Adverts in Nigeria need approval from ARCON, the Advertising Regulatory Council of Nigeria, before they run, and financial brands also have to keep claims about rates, returns and safety accurate and clear. We plan vetting time into every campaign timeline and write copy with compliance teams in mind, so launches don't stall at approval.

What kinds of financial brands do you work with?

Fintechs, mobile money operators, digital banks, pension fund administrators, insurers, lenders and other financial service providers.

Can you help us grow app downloads and sign-ups?

Yes. We run performance campaigns on Meta, Google and TikTok aimed at installs, completed sign-ups and first transactions, not just clicks, and pair them with creative and influencer content that builds the trust people need before they hand over their money.

How soon will we see results?

Paid campaigns start producing sign-ups within the first weeks, while brand awareness, organic social growth and search rankings build over three to six months. We agree targets with you at the start and report against them every month.

How do you market a fintech app in Nigeria?

Start with trust: clear proof that customers' money is safe, licensing information and real customer stories. Then combine app install ads on Meta, Google and TikTok, influencer content that explains the product simply, social media that answers questions quickly, and on-ground activity such as agent networks and events. Track sign-ups and first transactions, not just downloads.
