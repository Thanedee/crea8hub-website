03WhatsApp Business tips

Get more from every chat

A link brings people in. These free WhatsApp Business features help you reply faster and close more sales.

01

Set up your catalogue

Add your products with photos, prices and short descriptions. Customers can browse without asking "how much?" for every item, and you can send a product straight into a chat.

02

Save quick replies

Save answers to the questions you get every day: prices, delivery areas, account details, opening hours. Type "/" in a chat to pick one, then tweak it before you send.

03

Use greeting and away messages

A greeting message thanks new customers and tells them what to expect. An away message tells people when you'll reply if they write after hours, so they don't go elsewhere.

04

Label your chats

Use labels such as "New order", "Paid", "Delivered" and "Follow up" to keep track of each customer. Filter by label at the end of the day to see who still needs a reply.

04Questions

WhatsApp link FAQs

Does this work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. A wa.me link works the same way for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers. The chat opens in whichever app the customer has installed.

Is the generator really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up and no limit. Your link and QR code are made in your browser, so we don't store your number, and they keep working even if you never come back to this page.

Will the message be sent automatically?

No. The link opens a chat with your message already typed in. The customer can change it, and nothing is sent until they tap send.

Can I use a landline number?

Only if that landline is registered on WhatsApp Business, which lets you verify a number by voice call. If WhatsApp isn't set up on the number, the link will show an error. Test your link before you share it.

How do I add the link to my Instagram bio?

Copy your link, open Instagram, tap Edit profile, then Links, then Add external link. Paste it, give it a title such as "Chat on WhatsApp" and save.

Does the QR code expire?

No. The QR code simply holds your wa.me link, so it works for as long as your number is on WhatsApp. If you change your number, make a new code and reprint.
